Frequently asked questions

Quick help before you place a custom order.

Are the shades custom-fit for my car?

Choose your brand, model and year above, then include those details in your WhatsApp order. Our team will confirm the fitment details with you.

What is the difference between Half and Full Window Shades?

Half shades focus on partial front-window coverage and mirror visibility. Full shades offer full-height coverage with more privacy and sun blocking.

What is included in the Executive Combo Set?

The combo includes 2 front half shades and 2 rear full shades.

Is an advance payment required?

Yes. A Rs. 500 advance payment is required for custom orders. The team will confirm the next steps on WhatsApp.

What if I cannot find my exact car?

Message us on WhatsApp with your car brand, model and year and ask us to confirm whether a custom fit is available.
